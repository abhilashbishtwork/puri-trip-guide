8–17 Sep · Bhubaneswar & Puri

Full 10-day plan

Where you're staying

🏨 Bhubaneswar — nights of 8, 9 & 16 Sep

Pick a hotel within ~1–2 km of Biju Patnaik Airport (BBI) so Day 10's exit is a short cab ride, not a long one. Good options to compare: Mayfair Lagoon, Hotel Bijaya Palace (closest to BBI), Swosti Premium.

🏨 Puri — nights of 10–16 Sep

Look for a sea-facing property on Chakra Tirtha Road, close to both the beach and the temple. Good options to compare: Mayfair Heritage, Toshali Sands, Hans Coco Palms.
